A. Since both performers scored an identical 1.0, their nights were equally smooth, with roughly the same number of things going wrong backstage for each of them.
B. The Fen index is a rate, not a count -- it says nothing about how often correction was needed, so the matching score hides that Troupe B's performer carried a far heavier correction load.
