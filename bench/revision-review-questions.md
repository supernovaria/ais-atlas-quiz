**This is a verification review of a REVISED bench passage**, not a fresh review. The passage was reviewed, the orchestrator accepted findings (`private/reviews-decisions.md`), and an author revised it (`private/revision-log.md`). Your job is to check the revision, briefly. Answer these, and nothing else unless it is serious.

1. **Was each accepted finding actually addressed?** Go through `private/reviews-decisions.md` row by row. For each, say *addressed*, *partly*, or *not addressed*, with the passage text that shows it. Do not re-argue findings that were rejected.

2. **Did the revision introduce new problems?** In particular: after renaming coined terms, does every definition still match every use, including the worked example? Did a new or changed claim contradict an existing one? Did a new name give its own distinction away?

3. **Are the new tags honest?** Spot-check the claims the revision added or changed. Under the tie-break (disputed tags go the more guessable way), would you tag any differently?

4. **Is it ready for first use?** One line: *ready*, *ready with the following caveats*, or *not ready*, and why.

Keep it short. A verification review that re-litigates settled findings costs more than it finds.
